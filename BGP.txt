	route reflector :
		One or more routers are introduced as reflectors and the ibgp split horizon rule is abandoned. If ibgp gives an update, it creates the ability to teach others and the possibility of repetition is eliminated due to the existence of originator id + cluster id.

		When it receives the remote work number from router number 8 and AS, the oid is determined and the cluster id introduced by the network administrator is considered. If a loop occurs, according to the cid value, the advertisement will not take place, even if the router producing the bgp content receives its own packets again, due to the presence of the same cid, it will drop the packet.
