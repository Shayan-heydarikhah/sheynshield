# 1️⃣ NSE 1 — Threat Landscape

### Focus

Cybersecurity fundamentals and threat awareness.

### Learn

* Cybersecurity concepts
* Threat landscape
* Common attack types
* Security terminology
* Basic security awareness
* Threat actors
* Security risks

### Goal

```text
Understand the Problem
        ↓
Understand the Threat
        ↓
Understand Why Security Matters
```

---

# 3️⃣ NSE 3 — Fortinet Solution Knowledge

### Focus

Detailed understanding of Fortinet solutions and their positioning.

Learn to answer:

```text
What problem does this product solve?

Why is it required?

Where does it fit in the architecture?

What Fortinet product should be used?

How does it integrate with other products?
```

### Main Skill

```text
Requirement
    ↓
Architecture
    ↓
Fortinet Product
    ↓
Solution
```

# 6️⃣ NSE 6 — Specialist

### Focus

Become specialized in individual Fortinet security technologies.

Representative specialist domains:

```text
Advanced Security
      │
      ├── FortiWeb
      ├── FortiMail
      ├── FortiAuthenticator
      ├── FortiDDoS
      ├── FortiNAC
      ├── FortiAP
      ├── FortiWLC
      └── Wireless / Cloud Wireless
```

### Learning Strategy

Do not try to master every product simultaneously.

Instead:

```text
NSE 4
 │
 ├── FortiWeb Specialist
 │
 ├── FortiMail Specialist
 │
 ├── FortiNAC Specialist
 │
 ├── FortiAuthenticator Specialist
 │
 └── Wireless Specialist
```

Choose the specialist tracks that match your career direction.

# 8️⃣ NSE 8 — Expert

The expert level represents the highest level of Fortinet technical expertise.

The learning model becomes:

```text
Knowledge
   +
Architecture
   +
Troubleshooting
   +
Design
   +
Real-World Engineering
```

### Written Exam

Tests advanced theoretical and architectural knowledge.

### Practical Exam

Focuses on solving complex real-world scenarios in a lab environment.
